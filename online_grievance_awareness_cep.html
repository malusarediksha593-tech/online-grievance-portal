About the Project

Community Engagement Project (CEP)

What is a Grievance?

A grievance is a complaint or problem reported by a citizen about a public service or government service.

What is Online Redressal?

It is a digital method that allows people to submit complaints online and receive updates about their complaints.

Project Objective

To create awareness about online grievance systems and help citizens understand the basic complaint registration process.

Citizen Support

The website provides simple information and a demo complaint form for educational purposes.

Types of Grievances

Common problems that citizens may report

Roads & Potholes

Problems related to damaged roads, potholes and unsafe public roads.

Water Supply

Water shortage, leakage or other public water supply issues.

Garbage & Sanitation

Uncollected garbage, cleanliness and sanitation-related problems.

Streetlights

Non-working or damaged streetlights in public areas.

Health Services

Problems related to public health services and facilities.

Education

Issues related to public educational services and facilities.

Government Services

Problems related to public offices and government services.

How to Register a Complaint

Basic steps for using an online grievance system

1

Visit the Portal

Open the official online grievance portal provided by the concerned authority.

2

Register / Login

Create an account or log in if the portal requires registration.

3

Select Category

Choose the appropriate category for your complaint.

4

Enter Details

Provide the location and a clear description of the problem.

5

Upload Evidence

If permitted, upload a relevant photograph or document.

6

Submit & Track

Submit the complaint and save the complaint/reference number for tracking.

Project Conclusion

Online grievance redressal systems can provide a convenient digital channel for reporting public-service problems. Awareness is important because citizens need to know where to register complaints, what information to provide and how to track a complaint. This CEP website is designed to explain these steps in simple language and encourage informed use of online grievance services.
